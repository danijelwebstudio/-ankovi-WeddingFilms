BEHIND THE CAMERA

HI, I'M LAZAR.

I don't want your wedding to become a collection of beautiful shots with nothing holding them together.

For the past four years, I’ve been filming weddings with a documentary mindset and a cinematic eye. After working with photographers and agencies, I found the kind of work I wanted to make under my own name: films built around real moments, real voices and the feeling of the day.

I keep the number of weddings intentionally limited so I can stay close to every story — before, during and after the day. Based in Novi Sad and originally from Prijedor, I’ve filmed across Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina and Greece, and I’m open to stories wherever they take me.

THE APPROACH

A film begins before the camera is turned on.

01

BEFORE THE DAY

We talk through the schedule, locations, people, priorities and the moments that matter to you. When travel requires it, I may arrive a day or two earlier so the wedding day never depends on a delayed journey.

02

THE WEDDING DAY

I stay present throughout the day without turning it into a production set. Depending on the scale, I may work with one or two additional camera operators. Drone filming is available. We dress for the occasion and work discreetly.

03

THE STORY

Documentary storytelling, cinematic imagery and, when it fits, interview-driven storytelling. The point is not to record everything in order, but to build rhythm, emotion and meaning from what actually happened.

04

DELIVERY

Depending on the agreement, delivery may include a documentary film, teaser and short reels. Final films usually run from around 20 minutes to one hour, with the main delivery target within two months.

PORTFOLIO

SELECTED WEDDINGS

A small selection of stories. The films stay in focus; everything around them stays quiet.
